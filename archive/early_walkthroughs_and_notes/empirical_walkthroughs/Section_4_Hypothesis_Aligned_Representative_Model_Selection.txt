4.1 SECTION PURPOSE & OBJECTIVES
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This section documents the theoretical and methodological rationale for selecting the specific 
predictive models evaluated in this research. 

Rather than selecting algorithms arbitrarily or testing black-box libraries at random, each model 
was purposively chosen to serve as an archetype representing its broader modeling paradigm:
1. Deterministic Baselines (Status-Quo Airport Planning Practice)
2. Probabilistic / Data-Driven Machine Learning (Continuous Kernel Density Estimation)
3. Dynamic Hybrid Frameworks (Sequential Two-Stage Cyber-Physical Architecture)

This principled selection ensures that empirical differences in forecast performance directly test 
the core hypotheses regarding Robustness, Resilience, and Generalizability.

4.3 MATHEMATICAL FORMULATIONS OF THE THREE REPRESENTATIVE MODELS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Model 1 (M1 - Deterministic Linear Baseline):
   y_t = beta_0 + beta_1 * SchedFlights_t + sum_{k=1}^K [a_k sin(2*pi*k*t/24) + b_k cos(2*pi*k*t/24)] + epsilon_t
   - Assumes passenger arrival volume is contemporaneous with scheduled flight pushback times.
   - Contains no lead horizons, no passenger arrival spreading, and no operational delay feedback.
   - Scaled using M0 (Diurnal Naive Persistence: y_hat_t = y_{t-24}) to calculate MASE.

2. Model 2 (M3 - Probabilistic / Data-Driven Convolved ML):
   y_t = f(ConvolvedDemand_{t+1, t+2, t+3}, LoadFactor_t, DepDelay_{t-1}, CancelCount_{t-1}, DOW, Month)
   - Estimates passenger volume using continuous lognormal arrival density convolution across pre-departure leads.
   - Closed-form L2 regularization and gradient-boosted trees optimize objective without overfitting.
   - Strictly enforces causal delay feedback by lagging flight delays to t-1.

3. Model 3 (M5 - Sequential Two-Stage SARIMA-Tree Hybrid):
   - Stage 1 (Global Diurnal Schedule Model):
     y_hat_{Stage1, t} = beta_0 + beta_1 * ConvolvedDemand_t + HarmonicSeasonality(hour, dow, month)
   - Stage 2 (Non-Linear Residual Tree):
     Compute residual: e_t = y_t - y_hat_{Stage1, t}
     Train Tree Regressor on e_t using features: [DepDelay_{t-1}, Cancel_{t-1}, ConnectingRatio, TaxiOut_{t-1}]
     Yields residual forecast: e_hat_{Stage2, t}
   - Composite Forecast:
     y_hat_t = max(0, y_hat_{Stage1, t} + e_hat_{Stage2, t})
   - Rationale: The linear stage preserves physical long-term conservation laws, while the tree stage 
     captures sudden localized queue build-ups and weather delay shocks.

4.4 FEATURE ENGINEERING & LEAD-LAG ARRIVAL DECONVOLUTION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Empirical Evidence of Lead-Lag Asynchrony:
   Empirical regression of checkpoint throughput against flight departure horizons demonstrated 
   a profound physical asynchrony between takeoff times and checkpoint demand:

   +------------------------------------+------------+------------------+---------------------------+
   | Feature Horizon Representation     | Linear R^2 | Pearson Corr (r) | Empirical Slope (pax/flt) |
   +------------------------------------+------------+------------------+---------------------------+
   | Contemporaneous Departures (t)     | 0.1988     | 0.4459           | 38.42 pax/flight          |
   | Lead Horizon t+1 (1 hr pre-dep)    | 0.3723     | 0.6102           | 62.15 pax/flight          |
   | Lead Horizon t+2 (2 hr pre-dep)    | 0.4054     | 0.6367 (PEAK)    | 74.98 pax/flight (PEAK)   |
   | Lead Horizon t+3 (3 hr pre-dep)    | 0.3299     | 0.5744           | 51.20 pax/flight          |
   | Continuous Convolved Lead Demand   | 0.4878     | 0.6985           | 74.98 pax/flight          |
   | Convolved Demand * T-100 Load Fact | 0.4985     | 0.7061           | 90.56 pax/flight          |
   +------------------------------------+------------+------------------+---------------------------+

2. Mathematical Formulation of the Arrival Convolution:
   ConvolvedDemand_t = sum_{tau=1}^3 SchedSeats_{t+tau} * (1 - ConnectingRatio) * LoadFactor_t * f_arr(tau)
   Where f_arr(tau) is parameterized by an empirical lognormal kernel:
   tau ~ Lognormal(mu = 4.65, sigma = 0.32), mode at 90–120 minutes pre-departure.

3. Strict Causality and Zero Lookahead Bias:
   - Flight schedules (SchedSeats) are known months in advance.
   - Monthly load factors (T-100) are lagged.
   - Tactical flight delay metrics (DepDelay, TaxiOut, Cancellations) are strictly indexed at t-1, 
     preventing the model from using future flight outcomes to predict past checkpoint queues.
