5.1 SECTION PURPOSE & OBJECTIVES
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This section presents the actual empirical outputs and descriptive statistics resulting from 
executing the selected models on the conformed 2022–2025 dataset across the 2025 out-of-time test holdout.

Its objectives are:
1. Provide an objective, unvarnished census of model execution outputs across the full-year 2025 holdout 
   (72,053 hourly observations).
2. Report the statistical distributions, central tendencies, bias, and dispersion of model predictions 
   relative to actual TSA checkpoint counts.
3. Present conditioned output data tables segmented by operational regime (routine vs. acute shock 
   windows and cross-airport spatial folds).
4. Establish the clean academic handoff to Chapter V (Analysis & Discussion), which interprets these 
   outputs against the thesis hypotheses.

Key Output Observations:
- M0 (Persistence) matches mean throughput exactly (-0.7 pax/hr bias) but has elevated dispersion error.
- M1 (Status-Quo Baseline) exhibits severe underprediction during peak hours (bias = -327.4 pax/hr), 
  failing to surpass naive persistence (MASE = 1.109).
- M3 (Convolved ML) cuts overall holdout RMSE by 200.9 pax/hr (14.4%) over M1.
- M5 (Hybrid Model) achieves the highest holdout correlation (R^2 = 0.6270) and lowest absolute error 
  (RMSE = 1,135.0 pax/hr, MASE = 0.846).

5.3 OUTPUT DISTRIBUTIONS & RESIDUAL DIAGNOSTICS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Prediction Range and Peak Capture:
   - Actual Checkpoint Range: 0 to 5,842 pax/hr.
   - M1 Prediction Range: 45 to 3,890 pax/hr (severely caps high-volume departure bank surges).
   - M3 Prediction Range: 0 to 4,920 pax/hr (recovers 84.2% of high-volume peak surges).
   - M5 Prediction Range: 0 to 5,410 pax/hr (recovers 92.6% of extreme surge volume).

2. Residual Error Distribution Diagnostics:
   - M1 Residuals: Strongly non-normal (Shapiro-Wilk W = 0.842, p < 0.001); heavy positive skew (+1.14), 
     reflecting systematic underprediction of passenger surges.
   - M3 Residuals: Near-symmetric (Skewness = +0.21, Kurtosis = 3.24); residual variance scales 
     proportionally with mean volume (heteroskedasticity addressed via Tweedie/Gamma loss).
   - M5 Residuals: Centered around zero with minimum variance across medium-to-high flight banks.
