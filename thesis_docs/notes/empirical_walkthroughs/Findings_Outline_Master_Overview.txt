----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. CHAPTER PURPOSE & METHODOLOGICAL DEMARCATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Chapter IV is dedicated strictly to the objective, empirical presentation of findings and descriptive 
statistics derived from data engineering, purposive sample filtering, model specification, and model 
execution.

To maintain the highest standards of academic rigor and prevent scope creep:
- Chapter IV (Findings / Results) answers:
  "WHAT DO THE CONFORMED DATA AND MODEL RUNS FACTUALLY DEMONSTRATE?"
  It provides the unvarnished baseline statistics, complete post-ETL data health census, filtering 
  evidence, exclusivity proofs, filtered modeling dataset descriptives, architectural justifications, 
  and raw execution output tables.

- Chapter V (Analysis & Discussion) answers:
  "WHAT DO THESE FINDINGS MEAN FOR THE THESIS HYPOTHESES, EVALUATION MEASURES, AND AIRPORT OPERATIONS?"
  It analyzes and interprets the results across the three core dimensions: Robustness, Resilience, 
  and Generalizability, performing formal hypothesis testing (Diebold-Mariano) and evaluating trade-offs.
