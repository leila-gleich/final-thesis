1.1 SECTION PURPOSE & METHODOLOGICAL SCOPE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This section establishes the empirical baseline of the entire research warehouse immediately 
following raw data ingestion, automated cleaning, surrogate key transformation, and relational joining.

CRITICAL SCOPE DISTINCTION:
- Section 1 documents EVERY SINGLE DATA POINT post-ETL across the complete, nationwide commercial 
  aviation network (covering 25 candidate commercial airfields and 955 individual physical checkpoint lanes 
  over the continuous 7-year period from January 1, 2019 to December 31, 2025).
- Total Post-ETL Cleaned Warehouse Volume: Exactly 42,062,039 conformed records (3.5 GB DuckDB warehouse), 
  synthesized from 67,222,828 raw upstream government records (7.12 GB).
- The subsequent narrowing to the 9-airport experimental cohort occurs in Section 2 (The Filtering Pipeline), 
  and the descriptive statistics for that specific filtered modeling dataset are reported in Section 4.

1.4 DATA HYGIENE, ANOMALY REMEDIATION & MISSINGNESS RESOLUTION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Structural Zeros versus Sensor Dropouts:
   - Exactly 64,743 records (1.01% of cleaned post-ETL data) report zero passenger throughput.
   - Cross-referencing flight schedules confirmed that 98.6% of zero intervals occur during the early 
     morning non-operational window (00:00 to 03:59 local time).
   - Preserved as true physical lane closures rather than naively imputed with moving averages.

2. Spatial Key Resolution & Checkpoint Fingerprinting:
   - Upstream raw TSA logs contained 35,809 records with missing or corrupted airport codes.
   - Automated checkpoint fingerprinting matched checkpoint naming strings to recover 7,489 records.
   - The remaining 22,190 unresolvable records were isolated to a null surrogate key (airportId = 0, 
     airportMissing = 1), preventing them from aggregating into an artificial 9.71-million passenger 
     phantom airport.

3. Flight Delays and Advance vs. Tactical Cancellations:
   - 99.4% of unassigned aircraft tail numbers occurred on cancelled flights.
   - Advance cancellations (> 24 hours pre-departure) were purged from capacity calculations.
   - Tactical cancellations (< 2 hours pre-departure) were retained in passenger demand curves, 
     reflecting that affected travelers had already entered and cleared landside security checkpoints.
