### 2. Why Were These Drafts Superseded?

On October 10, 2026, the author (Leila Gleich) conducted an extensive editorial review and completed revised Word drafts (`ChpI v4.docx`, `ChpII v5.docx`, `ChpIII v4.docx`, `ChpIV v5.docx`, and `ChpV v1.docx`). 

The active **`final-thesis-v1`** suite (~15,811 words total) was compiled directly from those author revisions. It:
1. Streamlined the narrative by offloading technical intermediary proofs and derivations to the **14-letter Appendix suite (`Appendix v4.md` / Appendices A through N)**.
2. Formatted Chapter III to strictly match the canonical Embry-Riddle 5-part architecture (*Research Approach, Sample, Sources of Data, Validity, Treatment of Data*).
3. Eliminated computer science and physics laboratory jargon in favor of authentic commercial aviation operations terminology.
4. Conformed all 16 manuscript tables strictly to APA 7th Edition standards.

These archived extended drafts remain preserved here if you ever need to reference earlier phrasing, detailed EDA tables, or expanded literature review subsections.
